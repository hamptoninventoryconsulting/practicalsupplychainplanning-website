Safety Stock Simulator

A free educational tool for seeing how demand uncertainty and supply delay use the same inventory buffer.

How this works

Each teaching scenario uses the same average demand and the same average lead time. The only thing that changes is where the uncertainty comes from. That keeps the three results comparable.

The reorder point is expected demand during the average lead time, plus the safety stock you set. Each Monte Carlo trial draws a lead time, then a demand for every day of that wait. If demand during the wait exceeds the reorder point, that cycle stocks out.

The simulation runs in this browser. Individual runs are not stored on a server. Temporary choices stay in sessionStorage for this tab only and clear when the tab closes.

Scenario inputs are versioned in scenarios.json.
